            Repair and recycling

            Parts and repair help

            Before authorizing a repair, ask the manufacturer or repairer to confirm the fault, exact model/type, compatible part number, full quote and warranty coverage. Repera does not estimate repair costs from a photo.

            Find replacement parts

              If repair is not practical: responsible recycling

              A photo does not prove that a vacuum is beyond repair. Consider a qualified repair assessment before disposal. Recycling recovers materials; it does not eliminate all environmental impact.

              Find a recycling place nearby

              Share your location once, or enter a city or postcode. No ongoing tracking. Repera keeps it only in this tab, excludes it from Gemini and saved summaries, and clears it when the assessment changes. Opening the search link shares that location with Google Maps.

              Search results are not verified collection sites. Call ahead to confirm they accept your complete vacuum and any battery, plus fees and opening hours. For a damaged battery, ask about safe handling before transporting it.
